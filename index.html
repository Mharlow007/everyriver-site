One river, the whole story. EveryRiver brings scattered knowledge together, from public authorities and from its community, and starts from what brings you to the water. It works on a laptop, a PC and a phone.

Knowledge connected

Bringing the fragments together.

River and canal knowledge is scattered across maps, datasets, encyclopaedias, field observations and local experience. EveryRiver brings them to you as one, so you can easily understand and enjoy them more.

Information from Wikipedia, Wiktionary and iNaturalist is included within the atlas and guide.

Sources and provenance remain visible, so knowledge can be checked, understood and improved. Add your favourite books, explain how things work, and verify, update, challenge and edit, for the benefit of future generations.

Help us build the world’s greatest river atlas and guide.

EveryRiver is not a finished atlas being prepared behind closed doors. It is a library that will grow through the knowledge, experience and creativity of its community.

Roadmap

The first 40, then everyone.

EveryRiver opens first to 40 people who will help add to the atlas and tell us what they think. It opens to everyone after that. No date is set, and the newsletter is where we will say first.

  1. FirstThe first 40

    Forty people use EveryRiver, add what they know and tell us what works and what does not.

  2. ThenRefine and prepare

    We improve the experience, knowledge and contribution tools with what they tell us.

  3. After thatOpen to everyone

    No date is set. Newsletter subscribers hear first.
